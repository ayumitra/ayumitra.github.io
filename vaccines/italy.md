# Vaccination schedule in Italy (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Italy by age, from Ministero della Salute (Italy); Intesa Governo, Regioni e Province autonome ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Calendario nazionale vaccinale, PNPV 2023–2025 (Italy): children and adolescents

**Published by**
Ministero della Salute (Italy); Intesa Governo, Regioni e Province autonome

**Version**
Calendario nazionale vaccinale, Intesa Rep. atti n. 193/CSR of 2 August 2023 (G.U. Serie Generale n. 194, 21 August 2023, pp. 62–65). PNPV 2023–2025 extended to 31 December 2026 by Intesa Rep. atti n. 243/CSR of 18 December 2025; no newer national calendar found on 18 October 2026.

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 2 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1 · recommended up to 5 months; Rotavirus, dose 2 · recommended up to 5 months; Rotavirus, dose 3 (three-dose vaccine only) · depends on the vaccine used · recommended up to 5 months

- At 3 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 1

- At 4 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 5 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 2

- At 10 months | DTP, booster 1; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 1; Hib, booster; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), booster

- At 12 months | MMR, dose 1; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1; Meningococcal ACWY

- At 13 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), booster · recommended up to 15 months

- At 5 years | DTP, booster 2; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 2; MMR, dose 2; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 2

- At 11 years | HPV, dose 1; HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after HPV, dose 1

- At 12 years | DTP, booster 3 (adult-type dTaP) · recommended up to 18 years; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 3 · recommended up to 18 years; Meningococcal ACWY, adolescent dose · recommended up to 18 years
