# Vaccination schedule in Denmark (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Denmark by age, from Sundhedsstyrelsen (Danish Health Authority), with Statens Serum Institut ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Børnevaccinationsprogrammet (Denmark): childhood vaccination programme

**Published by**
Sundhedsstyrelsen (Danish Health Authority), with Statens Serum Institut

**Version**
Vejledning om gratis vaccination mod visse smitsomme sygdomme m.v., VEJ nr 9956 af 26/09/2025 (in force; under BEK nr 1139 af 17/09/2025); SSI "Det danske børnevaccinationsprogram" (edited 28 May 2026) with Tabel 1 (intervals); SSI vaccine pages (Pentavac 23 May 2025, Boostrix Polio 27 June 2024, Gardasil 9 11 February 2025) and "Rejsevaccination af børn". Retrieved 2026-10-18.

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 3 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Polio, dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 5 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Polio, dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 12 months | DTP, dose 3; Polio, dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3

- At 15 months | MMR (MFR), dose 1

- At 4 years | MMR (MFR), dose 2

- At 5 years | DTP, booster (with polio); Polio, booster

- At 12 years | HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 13 years; HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after HPV, dose 1
