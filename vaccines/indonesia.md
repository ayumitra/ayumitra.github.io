# Vaccination schedule in Indonesia (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Indonesia by age, from Kementerian Kesehatan Republik Indonesia (Ministry of Health) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Indonesia: Imunisasi Rutin Lengkap (national programme)

**Published by**
Kementerian Kesehatan Republik Indonesia (Ministry of Health)

**Version**
Keputusan Menteri Kesehatan HK.01.07/MENKES/1098/2024 tentang Jenis dan Jadwal Imunisasi Program (ditetapkan 1 Juli 2024), as amended by KMK HK.01.07/MENKES/35/2025 (21 January 2025: single-dose HPV); cross-checked with the Kemenkes release of 21 March 2025 and the Kemenkes hexavalent release of 22 December 2025

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | Hepatitis B, birth dose; BCG · recommended up to 1 month; Oral polio (OPV), dose 1 · recommended up to 1 month

- At 2 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Oral polio (OPV), dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1

- At 3 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Oral polio (OPV), dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 2

- At 4 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Oral polio (OPV), dose 4; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 3

- At 9 months | MR (measles-rubella), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2

- At 10 months | Japanese encephalitis (JE) · only in some areas

- At 12 months | Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3

- At 18 months | DTP, booster; Hib, booster; Hepatitis B, dose 4; MR (measles-rubella), dose 2

- At 7 years | MR (measles-rubella), grade 1 · recommended up to 8 years; DT (diphtheria-tetanus), grade 1 · recommended up to 8 years

- At 8 years | Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria), grade 2 · recommended up to 9 years

- At 11 years | Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria), grade 5 · recommended up to 12 years; HPV, single dose (girls aged 11) · for girls · recommended up to 12 years
