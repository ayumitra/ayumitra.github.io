# Vaccination schedule in the United Kingdom (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in the United Kingdom by age, from UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) and NHS England, on the advice of the Joint Committee on Vaccination and Immunisation (JCVI) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## UK NHS

**Published by**
UK Health Security Agency (UKHSA) and NHS England, on the advice of the Joint Committee on Vaccination and Immunisation (JCVI)

**Version**
Complete routine immunisation schedule from 1 September 2026 (UKHSA, updated 24 September 2026); children born on or after 1 January 2025

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 8 weeks | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 1; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 1; Hib (6-in-1), dose 1; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 1; Meningococcal B, dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1

- At 12 weeks | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 2; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 2; Hib (6-in-1), dose 2; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 2; Meningococcal B, dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 2

- At 16 weeks | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 3; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 3; Hib (6-in-1), dose 3; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 1 year | Pneumococcal (PCV), booster; Meningococcal B, booster; MMR (as MMRV), dose 1; Chickenpox (as MMRV), dose 1

- At 18 months | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 4; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 4; Hib (6-in-1), booster; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 4; MMR (as MMRV), dose 2; Chickenpox (as MMRV), dose 2

- At 40 months | DTaP pre-school booster (4-in-1); Polio pre-school booster (4-in-1)

- At 12 years | HPV, single dose · recommended up to 14 years

- At 14 years | Td teenage booster (3-in-1) · recommended up to 15 years; Polio teenage booster (3-in-1) · recommended up to 15 years; Meningococcal ACWY · recommended up to 15 years
