# Vaccination schedule in Germany (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Germany by age, from Ständige Impfkommission (STIKO) beim Robert Koch-Institut ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Germany STIKO

**Published by**
Ständige Impfkommission (STIKO) beim Robert Koch-Institut

**Version**
STIKO recommendations 2026, Epidemiologisches Bulletin 4/2026 (22 January 2026), Table 1 "Impfkalender 2026"; RKI edoc version 6 of 10 September 2026 (doi:10.25646/13636.6)

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 6 weeks | Rotavirus, dose 1 · recommended up to 2 months

- At 2 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 1

- At 3 months | Rotavirus, dose 2

- At 4 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 3 (RotaTeq only) · depends on the vaccine used

- At 11 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3; MMR, dose 1; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1

- At 12 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 3

- At 15 months | MMR, dose 2; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 2

- At 5 years | Tdap, booster 1 · recommended up to 6 years

- At 9 years | Tdap, booster 2 · recommended up to 16 years; Injectable polio (IPV), booster · recommended up to 16 years; HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 14 years; HPV, dose 2 · recommended up to 14 years

- At 12 years | Meningococcal ACWY · recommended up to 14 years
