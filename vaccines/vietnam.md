# Vaccination schedule in Vietnam (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Vietnam by age, from Bộ Y tế (Ministry of Health), Viet Nam; Cục Phòng bệnh ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Vietnam: Chương trình Tiêm chủng mở rộng (EPI)

**Published by**
Bộ Y tế (Ministry of Health), Viet Nam; Cục Phòng bệnh

**Version**
Thông tư 52/2025/TT-BYT ban hành danh mục bệnh truyền nhiễm, đối tượng và phạm vi phải sử dụng vắc xin, sinh phẩm y tế bắt buộc (31 December 2025; in force 15 February 2026; replaces Thông tư 10/2024/TT-BYT), Điều 1 and Điều 3; month ages of doses 2–3 from the schedule Viet Nam reported to WHO for 2025 (WHO Immunization Data portal, data committed 6 July 2026)

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | Hepatitis B, birth dose; BCG · recommended up to 1 month

- At 2 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Oral polio (OPV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1 · only in some areas; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2 · only in some areas · 2 months after Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 3 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Oral polio (OPV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 2

- At 4 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Oral polio (OPV), dose 3

- At 5 months | Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1

- At 9 months | Measles, dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2

- At 1 year | Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 1; Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 2 · 1 week after Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 1

- At 18 months | DTP, booster 1; MR (measles-rubella)

- At 2 years | Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 3

- At 7 years | Td (tetanus and reduced diphtheria), booster 2
