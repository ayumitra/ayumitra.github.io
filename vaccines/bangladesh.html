Vaccination schedule in Bangladesh (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Bangladesh by age, from Expanded Programme on Immunization, Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Bangladesh (sources). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

Expanded Programme on Immunization: routine schedule (Bangladesh)

Published by
Expanded Programme on Immunization, Directorate General of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of Bangladesh
Version
DGHS "EPI Vaccine Schedule" (children's routine schedule, file dated January 2018, posted on dghs.gov.bd December 2024) with: HPV single dose for all girls aged 10 from July 2025 (DGHS EPI letter of 28 July 2025); TCV at 15 months from 1 August 2026 (DGHS EPI notice of July 2026 as reported by BSS, and the Civil Surgeon's Office, Sylhet, notice of 15 July 2026). Cross-checked with WHO Immunization Data (2025 schedule, retrieved 2026-10-18).
Last checked
18 October 2026
AgeVaccines
At birth
  • BCG
At 6 weeks
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 1
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1
  • Oral polio (bOPV), dose 1
  • Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 1
At 10 weeks
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 2
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2
  • Oral polio (bOPV), dose 2
At 14 weeks
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 3
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3
  • Oral polio (bOPV), dose 3
  • Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 2
At 9 months
  • MR (measles-rubella), dose 1
At 15 months
  • MR (measles-rubella), dose 2
  • Typhoid conjugate (TCV)
At 10 years
  • HPV, single dose (girls aged 10) · for girls · recommended up to 11 years
