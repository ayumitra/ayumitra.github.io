# Vaccination schedule in the Netherlands (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in the Netherlands by age, from Rijksinstituut voor Volksgezondheid en Milieu (RIVM), Rijksvaccinatieprogramma ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Netherlands RVP

**Published by**
Rijksinstituut voor Volksgezondheid en Milieu (RIVM), Rijksvaccinatieprogramma

**Version**
RVP vaccination schedule from 2025 (Gezondheidsraad advice); professional guideline "Uitvoering Rijksvaccinatieprogramma 2026", published 31 December 2025, chapters 7 and 10

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 6 weeks | Rotavirus, dose 1 · recommended up to 9 weeks; DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), extra dose · optional · recommended up to 9 weeks; Injectable polio (IPV), extra dose · optional · recommended up to 9 weeks; Hib, extra dose · optional · recommended up to 9 weeks; Hepatitis B, extra dose · optional · recommended up to 9 weeks

- At 3 months | Rotavirus, dose 2; DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 5 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 12 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3

- At 14 months | MMR, dose 1 · recommended up to 15 months; Meningococcal ACWY, toddler · recommended up to 15 months

- At 30 months | MMR, dose 2 · recommended up to 43 months

- At 5 years | Tdap booster (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis) · recommended up to 6 years

- At 9 years | HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 11 years

- After an earlier dose | HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after the dose before

- At 13 years | Meningococcal ACWY, teenager · recommended up to 15 years

- At 14 years | Td-IPV booster (diphtheria, tetanus, polio); Injectable polio (IPV), booster
