# Vaccination schedule in Singapore (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Singapore by age, from Ministry of Health Singapore and the Communicable Diseases Agency (CDA); school-based doses by the Health Promotion Board ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Singapore NCIS

**Published by**
Ministry of Health Singapore and the Communicable Diseases Agency (CDA); school-based doses by the Health Promotion Board

**Version**
National Childhood Immunisation Schedule (from birth to age 17 years), updated 12 October 2026, with "Details of the vaccinations recommended under the NCIS"

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | BCG; Hepatitis B, birth dose

- At 2 months | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 1; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 1; Hib (6-in-1), dose 1; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 2

- At 4 months | DTaP (5-in-1), dose 2; Injectable polio (5-in-1), dose 2; Hib (5-in-1), dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV13), dose 1

- At 6 months | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 3; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 3; Hib (6-in-1), dose 3; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV13), dose 2; Influenza, first season dose 1 · recommended up to 5 years

- After an earlier dose | Influenza, first season dose 2 · 4 weeks after the dose before

- At 12 months | Pneumococcal (PCV13), booster; MMR, dose 1; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1

- At 15 months | MMR, dose 2 (as MMRV); Chickenpox (varicella), dose 2 (as MMRV)

- At 18 months | DTaP (5-in-1), booster 1; Injectable polio (5-in-1), booster 1; Hib (5-in-1), booster 1

- At 10 years | Tdap (Tdap-IPV), booster 2 · recommended up to 11 years; Injectable polio (Tdap-IPV), booster 2 · recommended up to 11 years

- At 12 years | HPV, dose 1 (girls) · for girls · recommended up to 13 years

- At 13 years | HPV, dose 2 (girls) · for girls · recommended up to 14 years
