Vaccination schedules by country

The childhood vaccination schedules Ayumitra follows, for 25 countries, by age. Each page names who publishes the schedule and links its sources.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

South Asia

  • India— IAP-ACVIP Recommended Immunization Schedule (2025), 0–18 years · Universal Immunization Programme: National Immunization Schedule
  • Pakistan— Expanded Programme on Immunization: routine schedule (Pakistan)
  • Bangladesh— Expanded Programme on Immunization: routine schedule (Bangladesh)
  • Sri Lanka— National Immunization Programme: schedule (Sri Lanka)
  • Nepal— National Immunization Programme: routine schedule (Nepal)

Southeast Asia

  • Thailand— Thailand EPI
  • Malaysia— Malaysia NIP
  • Singapore— Singapore NCIS
  • Indonesia— Indonesia: Imunisasi Rutin Lengkap (national programme)
  • Philippines— Philippines: National Immunization Program (DOH)
  • Vietnam— Vietnam: Chương trình Tiêm chủng mở rộng (EPI)

Europe

  • Germany— Germany STIKO
  • Switzerland— Switzerland BAG/EKIF
  • Netherlands— Netherlands RVP
  • Austria— Austria Impfplan
  • Belgium— Belgium SHC (CSS/HGR)
  • Sweden— Barnvaccinationsprogrammet (Sweden): national childhood programme
  • Norway— Barnevaksinasjonsprogrammet (Norway): childhood immunisation programme
  • Denmark— Børnevaccinationsprogrammet (Denmark): childhood vaccination programme
  • Finland— Kansallinen rokotusohjelma (Finland): national vaccination programme for children and adolescents
  • United Kingdom— UK NHS
  • France— Calendrier des vaccinations 2026 (France): enfants et adolescents
  • Italy— Calendario nazionale vaccinale, PNPV 2023–2025 (Italy): children and adolescents
  • Spain— Calendario común de vacunación e inmunización 2026 (Spain): children and adolescents

Americas

  • United States— USA CDC
