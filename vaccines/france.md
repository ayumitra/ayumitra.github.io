# Vaccination schedule in France (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in France by age, from Ministère chargé de la santé (France), after the opinion of the Haute Autorité de santé ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Calendrier des vaccinations 2026 (France): enfants et adolescents

**Published by**
Ministère chargé de la santé (France), after the opinion of the Haute Autorité de santé

**Version**
Calendrier des vaccinations et recommandations vaccinales 2026, July 2026 edition (93 pages; file 2026_07_calendrier_vaccinal-2026_a4_93p_v11.pdf): tableau 4.1, section 2 chapters and catch-up tableau 4.5.1

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | RSV antibody (nirsevimab), first RSV season · optional

- At 2 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1

- At 3 months | Rotavirus, dose 2; Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 1

- At 4 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 3 (RotaTeq only) · depends on the vaccine used

- At 5 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 2

- At 6 months | Meningococcal ACWY, dose 1

- At 11 months | DTP, booster 1; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 1; Hib, booster; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), booster

- At 12 months | MMR, dose 1; Meningococcal B (MenB), booster · recommended up to 15 months; Meningococcal ACWY, dose 2

- At 16 months | MMR, dose 2 · recommended up to 18 months

- At 6 years | DTP, booster 2; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 2

- At 11 years | DTP, booster 3 (reduced-dose dTcaP) · recommended up to 13 years; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 3 · recommended up to 13 years; HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 15 years; Meningococcal ACWY, adolescent dose · recommended up to 15 years

- After an earlier dose | HPV, dose 2 · 5 months after the dose before
