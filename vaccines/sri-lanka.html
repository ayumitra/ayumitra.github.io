Vaccination schedule in Sri Lanka (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Sri Lanka by age, from Epidemiology Unit, Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka (sources). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

National Immunization Programme: schedule (Sri Lanka)

Published by
Epidemiology Unit, Ministry of Health, Sri Lanka
Version
"Updated National Immunization Schedule - 2026" (Epidemiology Unit poster, file last modified 31 March 2026), with fIPV moved to 4 and 9 months as recommended by the National Immunization Summit 2025 (report of 18 August 2025). Cross-checked with WHO Immunization Data (2025 schedule, retrieved 2026-10-18).
Last checked
18 October 2026
AgeVaccines
At birth
  • BCG
At 2 months
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 1
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 1
At 4 months
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 2
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 2
  • Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 1
At 6 months
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 3
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 3
  • BCG, second dose (only if no scar) · optional
At 9 months
  • MMR, dose 1
  • Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 2
At 12 months
  • Japanese encephalitis (JE, live), single dose
At 18 months
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 4
  • DTP, dose 4
At 3 years
  • MMR, dose 2
At 5 years
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 5
  • DT (diphtheria-tetanus), dose 5
At 10 years
  • HPV, dose 1 (girls, Grade 6) · for girls
After an earlier dose
  • HPV, dose 2 (girls) · for girls · 6 months after the dose before
At 11 years
  • aTd (adult tetanus-diphtheria), Grade 7
