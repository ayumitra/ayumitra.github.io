# Vaccination schedule in Thailand (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Thailand by age, from Department of Disease Control (กรมควบคุมโรค), Ministry of Public Health, Thailand ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Thailand EPI

**Published by**
Department of Disease Control (กรมควบคุมโรค), Ministry of Public Health, Thailand

**Version**
กำหนดการให้วัคซีนตามแผนงานสร้างเสริมภูมิคุ้มกันโรค ปี 2566 (DDC, 2023), with 2 IPV + 3 OPV from 1 July 2023 (EPI Operations Standard 2568), PCV at 2, 4 and 12 months from 2 March 2026 (NHSO announcement No. 11/2569, in force 1 October 2026) and influenza for children 6 months to under 5 years (influenza guideline 2569); as of 18 October 2026

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | BCG; Hepatitis B, birth dose

- At 2 months | DTP (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 1; Hib (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 1; Hepatitis B (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 4 months | DTP (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 2; Hib (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 2; Hepatitis B (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 6 months | DTP (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 3; Hib (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 3; Hepatitis B (DTP-HB-Hib), dose 4; Oral polio (OPV), polio dose 3; Rotavirus, dose 3 (not after Rotarix) · depends on the vaccine used; Influenza, first season dose 1 · recommended up to 5 years

- After an earlier dose | Influenza, first season dose 2 · 4 weeks after the dose before; HPV, dose 2 (girls) · for girls · 6 months after the dose before

- At 9 months | MMR, dose 1 · recommended up to 12 months

- At 12 months | Japanese encephalitis (live JE), dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3 (booster)

- At 18 months | DTP, dose 4 (booster); Oral polio (OPV), polio dose 4; MMR, dose 2

- At 30 months | Japanese encephalitis (live JE), dose 2

- At 4 years | DTP, dose 5 (booster) · recommended up to 6 years; Oral polio (OPV), polio dose 5 · recommended up to 6 years

- At 10 years | HPV, dose 1 (girls) · for girls · recommended up to 13 years

- At 11 years | dT (tetanus and adult-type diphtheria) · recommended up to 13 years
