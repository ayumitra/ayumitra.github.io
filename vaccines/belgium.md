# Vaccination schedule in Belgium (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Belgium by age, from Conseil Supérieur de la Santé / Hoge Gezondheidsraad (Superior Health Council of Belgium); carried out by the Flemish Community (Departement Zorg, Kind en Gezin) and the Wallonia-Brussels Federation (ONE) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Belgium SHC (CSS/HGR)

**Published by**
Conseil Supérieur de la Santé / Hoge Gezondheidsraad (Superior Health Council of Belgium); carried out by the Flemish Community (Departement Zorg, Kind en Gezin) and the Wallonia-Brussels Federation (ONE)

**Version**
Advice 9761 "Calendrier vaccinal de base (2025)", validated 3 December 2025, published April 2026 (annex calendar); community calendars: Flanders "Basisvaccinatieschema voor kinderen" (2026), Wallonia-Brussels "Calendrier de vaccination 2025-2026" and "2026-2027" (from 1 November 2026)

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 8 weeks | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1

- At 12 weeks | Rotavirus, dose 2

- At 16 weeks | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 3 (3-dose vaccine only) · depends on the vaccine used

- At 12 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; MMR, dose 1

- At 13 months | Pneumococcal (PCV), booster · recommended up to 15 months; Meningococcal ACWY, small child · recommended up to 15 months

- At 5 years | DTaP-IPV booster (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis) · recommended up to 6 years; Injectable polio (IPV), booster · recommended up to 6 years

- At 7 years | MMR, dose 2 · recommended up to 9 years

- At 11 years | HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 13 years

- After an earlier dose | HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after the dose before

- At 15 years | Tdap booster (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis) · recommended up to 16 years; Meningococcal ACWY, adolescent · recommended up to 16 years
