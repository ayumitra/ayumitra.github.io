# Vaccination schedule in the Philippines (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in the Philippines by age, from Department of Health, Republic of the Philippines (National Immunization Program) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Philippines: National Immunization Program (DOH)

**Published by**
Department of Health, Republic of the Philippines (National Immunization Program)

**Version**
Schedule reported by the Philippines for 2025 (WHO/UNICEF Joint Reporting Form), WHO Immunization Data portal, data committed 30 April 2026, retrieved 18 October 2026; NIP catch-up limits and minimum ages from the PPS/PIDSP/PFV Childhood Immunization Schedule 2026 (November 2025)

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | BCG; Hepatitis B, birth dose

- At 6 weeks | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Oral polio (OPV), dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 10 weeks | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Oral polio (OPV), dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 14 weeks | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 4; Oral polio (OPV), dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1

- At 9 months | MMR, dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2

- At 12 months | MMR, dose 2

- At 6 years | MR (measles-rubella), grade 1 · recommended up to 8 years; Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria), grade 1 · recommended up to 8 years

- At 9 years | HPV, dose 1 (girls) · for girls · recommended up to 10 years; HPV, dose 2 (girls) · for girls · 6 months after HPV, dose 1 (girls)

- At 12 years | MR (measles-rubella), grade 7 · recommended up to 14 years; Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria), grade 7 · recommended up to 14 years
