# Vaccination schedule in Switzerland (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Switzerland by age, from Bundesamt für Gesundheit (BAG) and Eidgenössische Kommission für Impffragen (EKIF) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Switzerland BAG/EKIF

**Published by**
Bundesamt für Gesundheit (BAG) and Eidgenössische Kommission für Impffragen (EKIF)

**Version**
Schweizerischer Impfplan 2026, Stand Februar 2026 (published 23 February 2026; corrected version 16 April 2026), Table 1 "Synopsis" and chapters 1–3

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 2 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1 (complementary) · optional

- At 3 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 1 (complementary) · optional

- At 4 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 2 (complementary) · optional

- At 5 months | Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 2 (complementary) · optional

- At 9 months | MMR, dose 1; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1

- At 12 months | DTaP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3; MMR, dose 2; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 2; Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 3 (complementary) · optional · recommended up to 18 months; Meningococcal ACWY, small child (complementary) · optional · recommended up to 18 months

- At 3 years | Tick-borne encephalitis (TBE), dose 1 (risk areas) · only in some areas

- After an earlier dose | Tick-borne encephalitis (TBE), dose 2 (risk areas) · only in some areas · 1 month after the dose before; Tick-borne encephalitis (TBE), dose 3 (risk areas) · only in some areas · 6 months after the dose before; Tick-borne encephalitis (TBE), booster (risk areas) · only in some areas · 10 years after the dose before; HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after the dose before

- At 4 years | DTaP-IPV booster (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis) · recommended up to 7 years; Injectable polio (IPV), booster · recommended up to 7 years

- At 11 years | Tdap booster (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis) · recommended up to 15 years; HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 14 years; Meningococcal ACWY, adolescent (complementary) · optional · recommended up to 15 years; Meningococcal B (MenB), adolescent dose 1 (complementary) · optional · recommended up to 15 years; Meningococcal B (MenB), adolescent dose 2 (complementary) · optional · recommended up to 15 years
