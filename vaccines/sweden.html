Vaccination schedule in Sweden (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Sweden by age, from Folkhälsomyndigheten (Public Health Agency of Sweden) (sources). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

Barnvaccinationsprogrammet (Sweden): national childhood programme

Published by
Folkhälsomyndigheten (Public Health Agency of Sweden)
Version
HSLF-FS 2016:51 consolidated with HSLF-FS 2019:17 and 2020:25 (in force from 1 August 2020); programme page "Barnvaccinationsprogram", updated 16 April 2026; guidance "Vaccination av barn och ungdomar", 7th edition, art. no. 25173, updated 23 September 2025. Retrieved 2026-10-18.
Last checked
18 October 2026
AgeVaccines
At 6 weeks
  • BCG (tuberculosis), risk groups only · optional
  • Rotavirus, dose 1
At 3 months
  • Rotavirus, dose 2
  • DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1
  • Polio, dose 1
  • Hib, dose 1
  • Hepatitis B, dose 1
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1
At 5 months
  • Rotavirus, dose 3 (three-dose vaccine only) · depends on the vaccine used
  • DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2
  • Polio, dose 2
  • Hib, dose 2
  • Hepatitis B, dose 2
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2
At 12 months
  • DTP, dose 3 (booster)
  • Polio, dose 3 (booster)
  • Hib, dose 3 (booster)
  • Hepatitis B, dose 3
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3 (booster)
At 18 months
  • MMR (MPR), dose 1
At 5 years
  • DTP, dose 4 (booster, with polio)
  • Polio, dose 4 (booster)
At 7 years
  • MMR (MPR), dose 2 · recommended up to 9 years
At 11 years
  • HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 12 years
After an earlier dose
  • HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after the dose before
At 14 years
  • dTp, dose 5 (reduced-dose booster) · recommended up to 16 years
