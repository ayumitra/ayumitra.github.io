# Vaccination schedule in Finland (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Finland by age, from Terveyden ja hyvinvoinnin laitos (THL, Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Kansallinen rokotusohjelma (Finland): national vaccination programme for children and adolescents

**Published by**
Terveyden ja hyvinvoinnin laitos (THL, Finnish Institute for Health and Welfare)

**Version**
WHO Immunization Data, Finland vaccination schedule for 2025 (rows committed 19 May and 24 August 2026), cross-checked with THL's programme overview and THL professional guidance pages as quoted in search results (thl.fi blocked direct retrieval). Retrieved 2026-10-18.

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 2 months | Rotavirus, dose 1

- At 3 months | Rotavirus, dose 2; DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Polio, dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 5 months | Rotavirus, dose 3; DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Polio, dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 12 months | DTP, dose 3; Polio, dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3; MMR (MPR), dose 1 · recommended up to 18 months

- At 18 months | Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1

- At 4 years | DTP, booster 1 (with polio); Polio, booster

- At 6 years | MMR (as MMRV), dose 2; Chickenpox (as MMRV), dose 2

- At 10 years | HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 13 years; HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after HPV, dose 1

- At 14 years | dtap, booster 2 (reduced dose) · recommended up to 16 years
