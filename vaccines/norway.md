# Vaccination schedule in Norway (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Norway by age, from Folkehelseinstituttet (Norwegian Institute of Public Health), for the Ministry of Health and Care Services ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Barnevaksinasjonsprogrammet (Norway): childhood immunisation programme

**Published by**
Folkehelseinstituttet (Norwegian Institute of Public Health), for the Ministry of Health and Care Services

**Version**
FHI Vaksinasjonshåndboka: "Barnevaksinasjonsprogrammet" (last changed 16.06.2026), "Innhentingsvaksinasjon ved avvik fra det norske barnevaksinasjonsprogrammet" (26.05.2025), "Intervaller mellom vaksinedoser" (26.05.2025), rotavirus (26.05.2025) and HPV (13.03.2025) chapters. Retrieved 2026-10-18.

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At 6 weeks | BCG (tuberculosis), risk groups only · optional; Rotavirus, dose 1

- At 3 months | Rotavirus, dose 2; DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Polio, dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 5 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Polio, dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 12 months | DTP, dose 3; Polio, dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3

- At 15 months | MMR, dose 1

- At 7 years | DTP, booster 1 (with polio) · recommended up to 8 years; Polio, booster 1 · recommended up to 8 years

- At 11 years | MMR, dose 2 · recommended up to 12 years

- At 12 years | HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 13 years

- After an earlier dose | HPV, dose 2 · 6 months after the dose before

- At 15 years | dTP, booster 2 (reduced dose, with polio) · recommended up to 16 years; Polio, booster 2 · recommended up to 16 years
