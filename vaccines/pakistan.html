Vaccination schedule in Pakistan (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Pakistan by age, from Federal Directorate of Immunization, Ministry of National Health Services, Regulations and Coordination, Government of Pakistan (sources). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

Expanded Programme on Immunization: routine schedule (Pakistan)

Published by
Federal Directorate of Immunization, Ministry of National Health Services, Regulations and Coordination, Government of Pakistan
Version
FDI vaccination schedule chart "Vaccination_Schedule_2023" (epi.gov.pk, archived 18 September 2025) and National Immunization Policy 2022, Annexure I and section 4.11 (catch-up). HPV routine phase-in from WHO Pakistan (16 September 2025) and WHO Immunization Data (2025 schedule). epi.gov.pk was unreachable on 2026-10-18; pages were read from the Internet Archive.
Last checked
18 October 2026
AgeVaccines
At birth
  • BCG
  • Oral polio (OPV), birth dose
  • Hepatitis B, birth dose (where offered) · only in some areas
At 6 weeks
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 1
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 1
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1
  • Rotavirus, dose 1
At 10 weeks
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 2
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 2
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2
  • Rotavirus, dose 2
At 14 weeks
  • Oral polio (OPV), dose 3
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 3
  • Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3
  • Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1
At 9 months
  • Typhoid conjugate (TCV)
  • MR (measles-rubella), dose 1
  • Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2
At 15 months
  • MR (measles-rubella), dose 2
At 4 years
  • DPT booster (Punjab) · only in some areas · recommended up to 5 years
At 9 years
  • HPV, single dose (girls aged 9, where introduced) · for girls · only in some areas · recommended up to 10 years
