# Vaccination schedule in Spain (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Spain by age, from Consejo Interterritorial del Sistema Nacional de Salud; Ministerio de Sanidad (Spain) ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Calendario común de vacunación e inmunización 2026 (Spain): children and adolescents

**Published by**
Consejo Interterritorial del Sistema Nacional de Salud; Ministerio de Sanidad (Spain)

**Version**
Calendario común de vacunación e inmunización a lo largo de toda la vida, calendario recomendado año 2026, approved by the CISNS on 12 December 2025 (PDF dated December 2025). Rotavirus product details from the Ministry's "Recomendaciones de vacunación frente a rotavirus" (approved 18 January 2024, updated 29 February 2024).

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | RSV antibody (nirsevimab), first RSV season · optional

- At 2 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1; Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1 · recommended up to 6 months; Rotavirus, dose 2 · recommended up to 6 months; Rotavirus, dose 3 (RV5 courses only) · depends on the vaccine used · recommended up to 6 months

- At 4 months | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Meningococcal B (MenB), dose 2; Meningococcal C (MenC), dose 1

- At 11 months | DTP, booster 1; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 1; Hib, booster; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), booster

- At 12 months | MMR, dose 1; Meningococcal B (MenB), booster; Meningococcal C (MenC), dose 2

- At 15 months | Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1

- At 3 years | MMR, dose 2 · recommended up to 5 years; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 2 · recommended up to 5 years

- At 6 years | DTP, booster 2; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 2

- At 12 years | HPV, single dose; Meningococcal ACWY, 12 years

- At 14 years | Td (tetanus, diphtheria), 14 years
