Vaccination schedule in Nepal (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Nepal by age, from Family Welfare Division, Department of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Population, Government of Nepal (sources). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

National Immunization Programme: routine schedule (Nepal)

Published by
Family Welfare Division, Department of Health Services, Ministry of Health and Population, Government of Nepal
Version
NIP Reference Manual for Health Workers 2079 ("Final Revised 2023", posted by FWD 22 December 2023; schedule pp. 9–10, Delay Schedule p. 11) with: fIPV at 14 weeks and 9 months from 20 Kartik 2079 (FWD letters of November 2022); single-dose HPV for grade 6 girls and out-of-school girls aged 10, routine from FY 2082/83 (HPV Vaccine Service Guideline 2081, December 2024). Cross-checked with WHO Immunization Data (2025 schedule, retrieved 2026-10-18) and the DoHS Annual Health Report 2081/82 (June 2026).
Last checked
18 October 2026
AgeVaccines
At birth
  • BCG
At 6 weeks
  • Oral polio (bOPV), dose 1
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 1
  • Pneumococcal (PCV10), dose 1
  • Rotavirus (Rotarix), dose 1
At 10 weeks
  • Oral polio (bOPV), dose 2
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 2
  • Pneumococcal (PCV10), dose 2
  • Rotavirus (Rotarix), dose 2
At 14 weeks
  • Oral polio (bOPV), dose 3
  • Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 3
  • Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 1
At 9 months
  • MR (measles-rubella), dose 1
  • Pneumococcal (PCV10), dose 3
  • Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 2
At 12 months
  • Japanese encephalitis (JE, live), single dose
At 15 months
  • MR (measles-rubella), dose 2
  • Typhoid conjugate (TCV)
At 10 years
  • HPV, single dose (girls, grade 6 or aged 10) · for girls · recommended up to 11 years
