# Vaccination schedule in Malaysia (2026)

The childhood vaccination schedule in Malaysia by age, from Ministry of Health Malaysia (Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia), National Immunisation Programme ([sources]). In Ayumitra you can choose it for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## Malaysia NIP

**Published by**
Ministry of Health Malaysia (Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia), National Immunisation Programme

**Version**
Jadual Imunisasi Kebangsaan, KKM, as published on the MyGOV portal (page updated 28 January 2026); HPV single dose since 2023 (NITG); PCV schedule from the KKM PCV implementation manual (2020); as of 18 October 2026

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | BCG; Hepatitis B, birth dose

- At 2 months | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 1; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 1; Hib (6-in-1), dose 1; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 2

- At 3 months | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 2; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 2; Hib (6-in-1), dose 2; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 3

- At 4 months | Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 5 months | DTaP (6-in-1), dose 3; Injectable polio (6-in-1), dose 3; Hib (6-in-1), dose 3; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), dose 4

- At 6 months | Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2; Measles, early dose (Sabah only) · only in some areas

- At 9 months | MMR, dose 1; Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 1 (Sarawak only) · only in some areas

- At 12 months | MMR, dose 2

- At 15 months | Pneumococcal (PCV), booster

- At 18 months | DTaP (6-in-1), booster; Injectable polio (6-in-1), booster; Hib (6-in-1), booster; Hepatitis B (6-in-1), booster

- At 21 months | Japanese encephalitis (JE), booster (Sarawak only) · only in some areas

- At 7 years | DT (diphtheria, tetanus), booster at 7 years

- At 12 years | HPV, single dose (girls) · for girls · recommended up to 14 years

- At 15 years | Tetanus toxoid (TT), booster at 15 years
