# Vaccination schedule in India (2026)

Two childhood vaccination schedules for India by age: from Indian Academy of Pediatrics, Advisory Committee on Vaccines and Immunization Practices and from Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India ([sources]). In Ayumitra you choose one for each child.

Last checked 18 October 2026.

This page is general information, not medical advice. Follow your child’s doctor and your national vaccination programme.

## IAP-ACVIP Recommended Immunization Schedule (2025), 0–18 years

**Published by**
Indian Academy of Pediatrics, Advisory Committee on Vaccines and Immunization Practices

**Version**
2025 recommendations, finalized 22–23 November 2025; published online 9 April 2026 in Indian Pediatrics 2026;63:311–324 (Table 2 and Figure 1)

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | BCG; Oral polio (OPV), birth dose; Hepatitis B, birth dose

- At 6 weeks | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 1; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 1; Hib, dose 1; Hepatitis B, dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 10 weeks | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 2; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 2; Hib, dose 2; Hepatitis B, dose 3; Rotavirus, dose 2; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 14 weeks | DTP (diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis), dose 3; Injectable polio (IPV), dose 3; Hib, dose 3; Hepatitis B, dose 4 (permitted, not required) · optional; Rotavirus, dose 3 (not needed for RV1) · depends on the vaccine used; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 3

- At 6 months | Influenza, dose 1; Typhoid conjugate (TCV) · recommended up to 9 months

- At 7 months | Influenza, dose 2

- At 9 months | MMR, dose 1

- At 12 months | Hepatitis A, dose 1

- At 15 months | MMR, dose 2; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), booster

- At 16 months | DTP, booster 1 · recommended up to 18 months; Hib, booster · recommended up to 18 months; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 1 · recommended up to 18 months

- At 18 months | Hepatitis A, dose 2 (inactivated vaccine only) · depends on the vaccine used · recommended up to 19 months; Chickenpox (varicella), dose 2 · recommended up to 19 months

- At 4 years | DTP, booster 2 · recommended up to 6 years; Injectable polio (IPV), booster 2 · recommended up to 6 years; MMR, dose 3 · recommended up to 6 years

- At 9 years | HPV, dose 1 · recommended up to 15 years; HPV, dose 2 (boys) · for boys · 6 months after HPV, dose 1

- At 10 years | Tdap · recommended up to 12 years

- At 16 years | Td or Tdap · recommended up to 18 years

## Universal Immunization Programme: National Immunization Schedule

**Published by**
Ministry of Health and Family Welfare, Government of India

**Version**
National Immunization Schedule (MoHFW/NHM, undated PDF) with: fIPV third dose at 9 months from 1 January 2023 (MoHFW); PCV nationwide from 29 October 2021 (PIB); HPV for girls aged 14 from 28 February 2026 (PIB). Age-wise schedule cross-checked with the PIB backgrounder of 17 March 2026.

**Last checked**
18 October 2026

- Age | Vaccines

- At birth | BCG; Oral polio (OPV), birth dose; Hepatitis B, birth dose

- At 6 weeks | Oral polio (OPV), dose 1; Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 1; Rotavirus, dose 1; Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 1; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 1

- At 10 weeks | Oral polio (OPV), dose 2; Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 2

- At 14 weeks | Oral polio (OPV), dose 3; Pentavalent (DTP + Hep B + Hib), dose 3; Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 2; Rotavirus, dose 3; Pneumococcal (PCV), dose 2

- At 9 months | MR (measles-rubella), dose 1 · recommended up to 12 months; Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 1 · only in some areas · recommended up to 12 months; Pneumococcal (PCV), booster · recommended up to 12 months; Injectable polio (fractional IPV), dose 3

- At 16 months | MR (measles-rubella), dose 2 · recommended up to 24 months; Japanese encephalitis (JE), dose 2 · only in some areas · recommended up to 24 months; DPT, booster 1 · recommended up to 24 months; Oral polio (OPV), booster · recommended up to 24 months

- At 5 years | DPT, booster 2 · recommended up to 6 years

- At 10 years | Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria), 10 years

- At 14 years | HPV, single dose (girls aged 14) · for girls · recommended up to 15 years

- At 16 years | Td (tetanus and adult diphtheria), 16 years
