# Delete your Pro account

You can delete your Ayumitra Pro account in the app or on this page. It takes a minute and can’t be undone.

## What deleting does

- **Deleted from Ayumitra’s server:** your email address and the record of your plan.

- **Kept for 400 days, in some cases:** if you used the free trial or Pro this month, or a subscription is still running: a one-way fingerprint of your email address with its counts of use (numbers only), so the same address finds its subscription again and gets no second free trial and no fresh allowance.

- **Not touched:** your health records. They are only on your device. To delete them, delete them in the app, or delete the app or its data.

**Deleting the account does not stop the App Store or Google Play charging you.** If a subscription is running, cancel it in the store too:

iPhone and iPad: Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or [apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)

Android: Google Play › your profile picture › Payments and subscriptions, or [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)

## In the app

Open Ayumitra and go to **Settings › Ayumitra Pro › Delete Pro account**.

## On this page

Confirm your email address with a code, then delete the account.

This form needs JavaScript. Without it, delete the account in the app, or write to [support@ayumitra.app](mailto:support@ayumitra.app) from the account’s email address.

Questions? Write to [support@ayumitra.app](mailto:support@ayumitra.app). See also the [terms](https://ayumitra.app/terms.md) and the [privacy policy](https://ayumitra.app/privacy.md#pro).
