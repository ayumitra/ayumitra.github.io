Privacy policy

Ayumitra keeps your family’s health records on your phone, encrypted. They are never sent to us, unless you email us something yourself or use Ayumitra Pro, the optional subscription. This page explains what that means for the app and for this website.

In short

  • Unless you subscribe to Ayumitra Pro, there is no Ayumitra account and no Ayumitra server that receives your health records. We don’t share or sell them.
  • With Ayumitra Pro, what you send to the assistant goes through our server to an AI model that answers, and is not kept after the answer. The Pro account holds your email address, not your records.
  • The app stores everything in an encrypted vault on your phone or tablet, and on a device that can run it, its AI runs there too.
  • The app contacts other companies only for what you choose: downloading its AI model, built-in web search if you turn it on, a cloud AI model or web search with your own key, importing from Health Connect or Apple Health, and health advice for a country you travel to. Each is described below.
  • If you share a child’s profile with the other parent, your two phones exchange it directly on your Wi‑Fi. It never reaches us.
  • This website sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers.

The Ayumitra app

Your records stay on your phone

Profiles, reports, scans and photos, conversations, measurements, medicines, vaccines, cycle logs, reminders and everything else you add are stored in an encrypted vault on your phone. The key is kept in the phone’s secure storage. We have no copy of the key and no access to the vault.

The AI runs on your phone

On a phone or tablet that can run it, reading reports and answering questions happens on the device with an on-device model, and works without a connection. On one that can’t, the app keeps working without it, and answers come only from a cloud AI model you turn on (below).

Downloading the AI model

The first time you set up Ayumitra, it downloads its model files (about 2.6 GB and 165 MB) from Hugging Face, Inc. (USA). Like any download, this shares your phone’s IP address and the request with Hugging Face, which may process it in the USA. No health data, name or other record is sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: the download is needed for the app to work.

A cloud AI model, only if you turn it on

Cloud AI is off until you agree to it and turn it on. If you do, you add your own key for OpenAI, Anthropic, Google (the Gemini API) or OpenRouter. Your question, and the parts of your records needed to answer it, are then sent from your phone directly to the provider you chose, under your own account with them and their terms; if you choose to have reports read by the cloud model, photos of their pages are sent too. Unless you turn the privacy shield off, the names of the people in your profiles, and common other spellings of them, are replaced with made-up ones in the text before it is sent. Other names, such as a nickname or a doctor’s, aren’t changed, and names printed on a page’s photo can’t be. You can see the text that was sent. Nothing passes through us. OpenRouter, Inc. (USA) passes each request on to the company that runs the model you picked: for paid models the app asks OpenRouter to use only companies that don’t keep or train on what is sent; a free model may keep it, so the app asks you to agree to each free model separately. If your Google key comes from a project without billing set up (Google’s free tier), Google may also use what is sent to improve its products, and human reviewers may read it; Google says it does not do this when billing is set up. The app tells you this before you agree. These providers may process data in the USA. The legal basis is your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by turning cloud AI off in the app; nothing more is sent from then on.

Ayumitra Pro, only if you subscribe

Ayumitra Pro is an optional subscription, described in the terms. Without it, nothing in this section applies.

  • What you send. The questions, reports and photos you send to the assistant go from your phone through our server to an AI model that answers. The model is hosted by our cloud provider in the EU. What you send is not kept after the answer and is not used to train AI models.
  • The Pro account. It holds your email address, your plan and its state, which profiles used the plan (as keys that say nothing about the profile), and counts of requests. It holds no health records.
  • Signing in. You sign in with your email address and a 6-digit code. The email with the code is sent through our mail provider, Proton AG (Switzerland), which receives your address and the code.
  • Where the server runs. At Amazon Web Services in Frankfurt, Germany (Amazon Web Services EMEA SARL, Luxembourg).
  • Paying. Apple or Google sells the subscription and takes the payment, under their own privacy policies. The store tells us which plan you have, whether it is running, and when it renews or ends. We don’t receive your payment details.
  • A free month we give you. If you asked us for Ayumitra Pro, for example by joining a waiting list, we may give your email address a free month. For that we keep the address on our server together with where we got it from (for example “waiting list”). If you never sign in, both are deleted 90 days after the free month ends. Nothing is charged, and the month ends by itself.
  • How long. The account and its counts of requests are kept until you delete it. Server logs are kept for 14 days; they hold no email address, question or answer. If you delete the account after using the free trial, or while a subscription is still running, we keep a scrambled marker of the email address for 400 days, so the same address finds its subscription again and gets no second free trial. The marker is a one-way fingerprint; the address itself is not in it.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for the account and the service you subscribed to, and your explicit consent (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR) for the health details in what you send, which the app asks for before Pro answers. The marker left after deletion rests on our legitimate interest in preventing misuse of the free trial (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

You can delete the Pro account in the app (Settings › Ayumitra Pro › Delete Pro account) or on the delete page. That removes your email address, the plan record and the list of profile keys from our server; only the marker described above may stay. It does not cancel a subscription that is running: cancel that in the App Store or Google Play.

Built-in web search, only if you turn it on

Built-in search is off until you turn it on, during set-up or in Settings › Looking things up. When it is on and no search key is saved, or you pick built-in search, the app can look up general public information, such as what a test measures, without any key: it sends the search words from your phone directly to MedlinePlus, run by the U.S. National Library of Medicine (USA), and to Wikipedia, run by the Wikimedia Foundation, Inc. (USA). Nothing passes through us. The app writes the search words itself and is designed to leave out names, dates and other personal details. Both services receive your IP address and the search words, under their own privacy policies. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR where a search could reveal something about health), given by turning it on; the app keeps the time you did. Adding a search key (below) also turns web search on. Turn it off to stop; nothing more is sent from then on.

Web search with your own key

If you add a key for Brave Search or Tavily, the app can look up general public information, such as a medicine’s label or a guideline, with that service: Brave Software, Inc. (USA), or AlphaAI Technologies Inc., doing business as Tavily (USA). With both keys saved, each search goes to the one you picked, never to both. The app writes the search words itself and is designed to leave out names, dates and other personal details. The service receives your IP address and the search words; Tavily says it may keep the questions it receives and use them to improve its service. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR where a search could reveal something about health), given by adding the key. Removing the key stops searches with that service; while web search is on, built-in search answers instead. To stop all searching, turn web search off in Settings › Looking things up.

Health Connect and Apple Health

Only when you tap Import and allow it, the app reads the last 30 days of weight, height, blood pressure, oxygen, temperature, heart rate, resting heart rate, blood sugar, daily steps and sleep from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone and iPad) into the profile you choose, on the device. Each reading keeps the name of the app that recorded it.

If you leave “Keep checking for new readings” on, the app then reads new readings into that profile by itself while it is open. On Android, if you also allow Health Connect’s reading in the background, it checks about once an hour while it is closed; this is a separate permission you can withdraw in Health Connect at any time. Turning Keep checking off, deleting that profile or deleting everything stops it.

It is read only: nothing is written back to Health Connect or Apple Health. What is read stays in the encrypted vault on the phone and goes to a cloud model only if you have turned cloud AI on.

Travelling abroad, only if you turn it on

When you turn on Travelling for a country, when you change the country, when you open Travelling a day or more after the last check, and when you tap Check again, the app downloads the World Health Organization’s list of disease outbreak news (WHO, Switzerland) and the U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention’s travel page for that country (CDC, USA). Like any download, this shares your phone’s IP address and the request with them; the CDC address names the country. Nothing about your family is sent. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR: the download is needed for the feature you turned on. Turn Travelling off to stop.

Sharing a child’s profile, only if you set it up

You can share a child’s profile with one other person you live with, usually the other parent, after you both agree on your phones with a one-time code. The child’s record (reports, measurements, medicines, vaccines, appointments, reminders and health memory, with who added each item) then goes directly between your two phones on the same Wi‑Fi or one phone’s hotspot, encrypted. It never passes through us or any cloud service. Your chats, settings, keys and other profiles stay on your own phone. What has reached the other phone stays there even if you stop sharing, and either of you can stop managing the profile at any time.

Backups

Backups are encrypted with a six-word passphrase that only you have. For now, the app saves them only to a folder on your device that you choose, never to iCloud Drive or another cloud drive. A backup kept on the device is lost if the device is lost or reset. Without your six words nobody can open a backup, including us.

Exports, sharing and emails you send

When you export or share a file, it goes only where you choose, and the file itself isn’t encrypted. If you send text to ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, WhatsApp or another app, for example to ask for a second opinion, the app opens that app with the text you reviewed, and that company’s own terms apply. Opening an appointment’s address hands the address to Apple Maps or Google Maps. If you report an answer or send feedback from the app, it opens your own email app with a draft to appfeedback@ayumitra.app that you can read and change before you send it; a feedback draft carries only the app’s version and whether it is Android, iPhone or iPad. The app may ask you to rate it. What it counts to choose a calm moment (the days it was opened and how often it worked for you) stays on your phone and is never sent; choosing to rate opens Google Play, or Apple’s own rating sheet on iPhone and iPad, under their terms.

Permissions

The app asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it: the camera and photos to add reports, the microphone for voice input (speech is recognised on the phone), notifications for reminders, health data for imports, and the local network to share a child’s profile with the other parent’s phone. Call buttons open your phone’s own dialler.

No tracking

The app has no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking code from other companies.

Children

A child’s profile is created and managed by a parent or carer on their own phone, and stays on that phone unless they share it with the other parent’s phone (see above).

Deleting your data

You can delete a report, a conversation, a profile or everything from within the app. Uninstalling the app removes its data from the phone. Backups you made stay where you saved them until you delete them. A Ayumitra Pro account is deleted separately, in the app or on the delete page.

This website

Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages by GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit, GitHub logs your IP address and stores it for security purposes, along with the usual details of a request (the page and the time), under the GitHub Privacy Statement. This is needed to deliver the site and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a working, secure website). GitHub may process it in the USA; for transfers out of the EEA it relies on the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework and the EU’s standard contractual clauses. We don’t receive these logs.

No cookies, no analytics, nothing from other servers

The site stores nothing on your device and reads nothing from it, so no consent banner is needed. Fonts and images are served from this site, not loaded from elsewhere.

Email

If you email us, we use your address and message to reply (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when it concerns the app you use, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f)). A reported answer contains the answer’s text and may say something about someone’s health: we use it only to look into the report, with the explicit consent you give by sending it (Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR). We delete messages when they are no longer needed, unless the law requires us to keep them.

Contact form

The contact form sends your name, your email address, the subject and your message, and a company name and phone number if you give them. They go through our server (Amazon Web Services, Frankfurt, Germany) and our mail provider Proton AG (Switzerland) to our support mailbox. The server keeps none of it. It keeps only a count per network address for two days, as a one-way fingerprint, to stop spam. We use what you send to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR when it is about a contract or a step towards one, otherwise Art. 6(1)(f), our legitimate interest in answering). The message stays in the mailbox as long as we need it to answer, then we delete it.

App stores

The download links take you to Apple’s App Store or Google Play. Their own privacy policies apply there.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access (Art. 15), correction (Art. 16), deletion (Art. 17), restriction (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and to object (Art. 21), and to withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)). Write to us at support@ayumitra.app.

Because your health records exist only on your phone, you see, correct, export and delete them yourself in the app. We can’t do it for you, because we don’t hold them. What a Pro account holds is listed under Ayumitra Pro.

You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the one responsible for us: Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.
