Adding your API keys

Ayumitra works fully on your phone without any keys. Keys are optional: one turns on web search for general questions, the others let you use a cloud model of your choice.

Your keys stay on your phone. They’re kept in the phone’s secure storage and are never sent to Ayumitra. Saving a key sends nothing; it’s only used when you choose web search or a cloud model. You pay the provider directly.

Turn on web search with Brave Search or Tavily

With a key for Brave Search or Tavily, Ayumitra can look up general health information online, such as “what is ferritin”. Only general, public questions are sent. Your reports, names and conversations stay on your phone. Web search doesn’t need cloud AI.

  1. Get a key

    Either one works. For Brave Search, sign up for the Brave Search API, choose a plan and copy your key from the dashboard. For Tavily, sign up at app.tavily.com, which has a free plan, and copy your key. Tavily says it may keep the questions it receives to improve its search.

  2. Open Looking things up

    In Ayumitra, open Settings and find Looking things up. Tap Add next to Brave Search or Tavily.

  3. Paste and save

    Paste the key into API key and tap Save key. You’ll see “Tavily key saved. Web search uses it now.”

  4. Check it’s on

    It now says On · searches with your Tavily key (or your Brave Search key). Ayumitra searches on its own when a question needs it. With both keys saved, the one saved last searches; tap the circle beside the other to switch.

Use a cloud model of your choice

By default, every answer and every report is handled on your phone. If you’d like a larger model, add a key from OpenAI, Anthropic or OpenRouter. Cloud stays off until you turn it on.

  1. Open Cloud AI

    Open Settings, then Cloud AI.

  2. Save the key

    Choose the company, tap Add key, paste the key and tap Save key. Saving a key sends nothing and turns nothing on.

  3. Read the agreement

    Tap Read the agreement and turn on. Cloud AI turns on only once you agree; it covers each company, and a free model asks for one more agreement first.

  4. Pick your model

    In a chat, tap On this phone · Gemma 4 at the top and pick your cloud model. For reports, pick it under Read reports and photos with when you add one. You can switch back at any time.

Privacy shield. With it on, real names are swapped for imaginary ones on your phone before anything goes to a cloud model, and swapped back in replies. Names printed on page images can’t be swapped.

What a cloud model receives. Chat sends your messages and the health details that matter for the question. Reading a report also sends page images and extracted text. The provider’s charges and retention policies apply. With OpenRouter, free models may keep or learn from what you send, so a paid model is the safer choice for health details.

Replace or remove a key

A saved key shows Saved on this phone, with Replace to paste a new one and Remove. AI companies’ keys are in Settings › Cloud AI; removing one takes chat and reading back to this phone. Brave Search and Tavily are under Settings › Looking things up; removing the one in use hands searching to the other, and removing both turns web search off.

Questions

Do I need any key to use Ayumitra?

No. Reading reports, search, answers, medicines and vaccines all work on your phone without a key.

Does saving a key send my health data anywhere?

No. Saving a key sends nothing. Data is only sent when you choose a cloud model, after a one-time notice, or when web search sends a general question.

Who do I pay?

The provider whose key you use, on their pricing. Ayumitra doesn’t add a charge.

Something isn’t working

Check the key was copied in full, and that your provider account has an active plan or credit. If a cloud request fails, Ayumitra stops and tells you; it never switches provider by itself.
