## Zero data retention

Web search with Brave is your choice, off until you turn it on: Brave keeps a record of the search words for up to 90 days. We have asked Brave for zero data retention; until we have it, that record applies.

## Why Pro costs money

Our aim is that every feature of Ayumitra stays free, always. But the AI that runs on a phone can’t yet match the leading AI models. Pro is for anyone who wants a stronger AI without setting up keys.

### What it pays for

The AI that answers, the web searches it makes and the servers in between all cost money. Pro covers these costs.

### What you get for it

Your monthly allowance, with web searches on top, is worth about twice what we receive from a monthly subscription, after the store’s fee and taxes. On the yearly plan, about three times.

### How it adds up

To be open about it: not everyone uses their whole allowance, so those who use less help pay for those who use more. Together, that covers what it costs to run Ayumitra each day.

## Questions

Do I need Pro to use Ayumitra?

No. The app is free and every feature works without paying. Its AI can run on the phone itself, or with your own key for a cloud AI provider.

Is there a limit on questions?

Pro has one monthly allowance for the whole account: 2,000 requests to the AI, about 650 questions. Reading reports and photos, and Think deeper, use more; web searches don’t count. The app shows how much is used. If it runs out, the app answers on the device until it starts fresh the next month. The 7-day free trial has 500 requests. The [terms](https://ayumitra.app/terms.md#pro) have the details.

What happens to my questions?

They leave the device for the answer. They aren’t kept afterwards and aren’t used to train AI. Your family’s names are swapped for made-up ones before anything is sent.

What if Pro can’t be reached?

The app answers on the device instead, and says why.

Can my partner use Pro too?

Yes. Sign in with the same email on their phone or tablet. Pro answers for every profile on every device signed in, and they all share the one monthly allowance.

How do I cancel?

In the App Store or Google Play, at any time. Pro runs until the time you paid for ends.

Why is the price different in my store?

The prices here are for Germany and include VAT. The store shows the price for your country.

Does Pro make Ayumitra a doctor?

No. With or without Pro, Ayumitra isn’t a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, and it never recommends medicines or doses.
