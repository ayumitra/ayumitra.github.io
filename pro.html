Questions

Do I need Pro to use Ayumitra?

No. The app is free and every feature works without paying. Its AI can run on the phone itself, or with your own key for a cloud AI provider.

Is there a limit on questions?

There’s no monthly limit on questions, within fair use. The terms say what fair use means.

What happens to my questions?

They leave the device for the answer. They aren’t kept afterwards and aren’t used to train AI. Your family’s names are swapped for made-up ones before anything is sent.

What if Pro can’t be reached?

The app answers on the device instead, and says why.

Can I use one plan on two devices?

Each plan is for one device. Solo covers 1 profile, and Family up to 4.

How do I cancel?

In the App Store or Google Play, at any time. Pro runs until the time you paid for ends.

Why is the price different in my store?

The prices here are for Germany and include VAT. The store shows the price for your country.

Does Pro make Ayumitra a doctor?

No. With or without Pro, Ayumitra isn’t a medical device. It doesn’t diagnose, and it never recommends medicines or doses.
