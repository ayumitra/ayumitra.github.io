How Ayumitra checks every answer

Ayumitra checks every question itself, before and after the AI answers. The checks are part of the app, so they apply whichever AI answers: the model on the phone, a cloud AI you set up with your own key, or Pro.

The checks lower the risk. They can’t catch everything. What they can’t do

Warning signs get a card from the app

If a message describes signs of an emergency, the app itself puts an urgent-care card above the answer. The card shows the emergency number of the country you’re in, with a button to call. The app doesn’t wait for the AI to notice.

Some of the signs it looks for:

  • Chest pain with sweating
  • Signs of a stroke
  • A baby under three months with a fever
  • A child who swallowed tablets

Thoughts of self-harm get a crisis line

If a message speaks of self-harm, the app shows the crisis line of your country.

It never gives methods or amounts, however the question is framed. It looks at what a message means, not only at its words.

  • USA988
  • United KingdomSamaritans116 123
  • GermanyTelefonSeelsorge0800 111 0 111
  • IndiaTele-MANAS14416
  • France3114
  • Spain024
  • Netherlands113

It explains. It doesn’t prescribe or diagnose.

If an AI answer contains a dose, or advice to start, stop or change a medicine, the app takes it out before it reaches the screen.

  • Medicines

    It doesExplain what a medicine is for and how it’s usually used.

    It doesn’tSuggest a medicine, give a dose for anyone, or say to start, stop or change one. It points to your doctor, your pharmacist or the label.

  • Illnesses

    It doesExplain, and say who can examine you.

    It doesn’tTell you that you have an illness, or that you don’t.

The same checks for everyone

Whichever AI answers

The checks are the app’s own. They run with the model on the phone, with a cloud AI you set up with your own key, and with Pro.

In six languages

The checks work in English, Hindi, German, French, Spanish and Dutch.

A note before the first answer

Every person reads and ticks a short note first: Ayumitra is a family health record app, not a doctor and not a medical device. Answers are written by AI, can be wrong, and are not medical advice.

Calm by default

An ordinary cold gets a calm answer. The urgent card is only for warning signs.

Tested before every release

A release goes out only when every test below passes. Passing them doesn’t mean the checks catch everything.

  • More than 70 hard situations

    A fixed set: thoughts of self-harm said plainly and veiled, “how much would harm” dressed up as fiction or as a professional’s question, dose questions for babies and adults, stopping medicines, and emergencies played down.

  • Each asked several times

    Every situation is asked several times, not once.

  • Again, with a badly behaved AI

    The same situations are run with an AI made to behave badly on purpose, to check that the app’s own guards hold.

  • All of them must pass

    If one fails, the release waits.

What the checks can’t do

  • Catch everything. The checks lower the risk. They can miss a warning sign, and an answer can still be wrong.
  • Make Ayumitra a doctor. It isn’t a doctor and it isn’t a medical device. Its answers are not medical advice.
  • Stand in for an emergency service. Ayumitra isn’t one. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

What applies when you use the app is in the terms.
