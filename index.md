# A health companion that knows your family.

Ayumitra keeps each person’s reports, medicines, vaccines and appointments, and the readings their phone and watch record, on your phone. Ask anything in plain words and see where every answer came from.

**iPhone and iPad** Coming soon to the App Store

[**Android** Get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.ayumitra.ayumitra)

The AI runs on your phone. No account, no ads.

## Watch it work

Four things Ayumitra does every day, shown with sample records.

### Reads a report from a photo

Photograph a lab report or add a PDF. It’s read on the phone, with each value, unit and date kept.

### Answers with the source

Ask in plain words. It says what it understood, shows each step, and the answer points to the report it came from.

### Turns a prescription into reminders

The course and its dose times are read from the photo. Nothing is added until you check it and say so.

### Puts warning signs first

When a message describes warning signs, the app’s own check shows what to do and your local emergency number before any answer. It can miss things.

## Readings come in by themselves

Import once from Health Connect or Apple Health, and new readings from the phone, the watch and the meters you already use keep coming into the right person’s record: blood pressure, blood sugar, oxygen, weight, temperature, heart rate, steps and sleep.

### It keeps checking

While Ayumitra is open, it looks for new readings by itself. On Android it can also check about once an hour while it’s closed, if you allow it in Health Connect.

### Named, and never twice

Each reading says which app recorded it, and one app’s same reading is kept once. A night two apps both recorded shows under each, and counts once.

### Into the right person

Readings go into the profile you choose and are read on the phone only. Nothing is written back. Picked the wrong person? Undo takes the import back.

## One record for each person in the family

Add a profile for each person. Their records, reminders and answers stay apart.

### Reports and scans

Lab reports, prescriptions and scans are read on the phone, so you can find any test, value or date.

### Search by meaning

Search your reports in your own words, or ask Ayumitra to find it for you. EmbeddingGemma, Google’s open model for search, runs on the phone and matches what you mean, not only the exact word.

### Medicines

Photograph a prescription and the course and its reminders are set up for you to check.

### Vaccines

Your country’s schedule from birth, with what’s done and what’s due next.

### Measurements

Temperature, blood pressure, sugar, weight, heart rate and more: logged with a tap, told in chat or brought in from the phone. Every entry can be corrected.

### Sleep

Each night with when it began and ended, from the watch or logged yourself, and a chart of the nights.

### Growth

Height and weight against the WHO growth charts, from birth to 19.

### Cycle

Periods with the next one as a range of days, never a guess, and states like pregnancy that pause predictions.

### Visits

Appointments and check-ins, and a brief to take to the doctor, written from the records.

### Shared with the other parent

Share a child’s record with the other parent’s phone. The two phones catch up on your home Wi‑Fi, never through a server, and each of you keeps your own chats.

### Health memory

One timeline of what happened, linked across reports and conversations, kept for life.

## Food, logged in a moment

Photograph a meal, scan a packaged food’s barcode or say what you ate, on the Food page or in chat. Ayumitra lists the foods and works out the calories and nutrients; you check them before anything is saved.

- English Lunch was 2 rotis, dal and a bowl of rice

- Hindi नाश्ते में पोहा और चाय

- German Zwei Brötchen mit Käse zum Frühstück

- Dutch Boterham met kaas en een glas melk

- French Un croissant et un café crème ce matin

- Spanish Lentejas con pan para comer

### From food tables and labels

The numbers come from food tables kept in the app, built from official data, or from the pack’s label through Open Food Facts. Every figure says “about”.

### No targets, no judging

There is no calorie goal, and nothing is ever called too much or too little. On a child’s profile, only the foods are kept.

### Linked to your own results

When a meal is high in saturated fat, sugars or salt and your latest result is above its range, such as LDL cholesterol, it says so calmly, with the date.

### A whole day at once

Say breakfast, lunch and dinner in one message, or send a photo of each. Every meal is kept on its own, at its own time.

### It says what it understood

The first step says what it took your question to mean. If it can’t tell, it asks you one short question instead of guessing.

### A source for every answer

You see each step as it works, and each answer points to the report, reading or conversation it came from. Tap it to open the report at that line.

### Warning signs come first

If a message describes warning signs, the app’s own check shows what to do and the number to call, before the answer. [How the app checks answers]

### Wherever you are

Each person has their own suitcase: while they travel, the emergency numbers shown for them are the country’s they’re in, with WHO’s and CDC’s health advice for it.

## The app checks every answer itself

**Ayumitra isn’t just an app. It’s a harness for AI-first health:** around whichever AI answers (the model on your phone, a cloud AI with your own key, or Pro), the app itself does the checking, the remembering and the record-keeping. It checks every question before and after the AI answers.

You ask

### Your question

In English, Hindi, German, French, Spanish or Dutch. The checks work in all six.

The app, before the answer

### Looks for warning signs

Signs of an emergency get an urgent-care card with your country’s emergency number. Thoughts of self-harm get your country’s crisis line.

The AI

### Explains, without a verdict

It explains and says who can examine you. It doesn’t say you have an illness, or that you don’t. An ordinary cold gets a calm answer.

The app, after the answer

### Takes out doses

If the answer contains a dose, or advice to start, stop or change a medicine, the app takes it out before it reaches the screen.

**The checks lower the risk. They can’t catch everything.** Before every release they are tested with more than 80 hard situations. [How Ayumitra checks every answer](https://ayumitra.app/safety.md)

## Your records are yours to control

There’s no account and no Ayumitra server holding your health records. In practice:

### Stored on your phone, encrypted

Records, photos and conversations are kept in an encrypted vault on the phone.

### Read on your phone

On a phone that can run it, the AI that reads reports and answers questions runs on the phone itself, and works without a connection.

### Health apps, read only

Health Connect and Apple Health are read on the phone, into the profile you choose. Nothing is written back to them.

### Cloud only if you choose

You can use OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter with your own key. It stays off until you agree and turn it on. The names of the people in your profiles, and common other spellings of them, are swapped for made-up ones first. Nicknames and other people’s names aren’t changed, and names printed on a photographed report can’t be.

### See what was sent

With a cloud model on, Settings shows the exact text sent this session, with the app’s own check for your family’s names in it.

### A health library in the app

General questions draw on MedlinePlus’s health topics in English and Spanish, kept in the app and read offline. Web search stays off until you turn it on, and the app lets only a general question through.

### Shared only between your phones

A child’s record shared with the other parent goes straight from phone to phone on your Wi‑Fi, encrypted. Chats are never shared.

### Backups only you can open

Backups stay on your device for now, encrypted with six words that only you keep. Without them nobody can open a backup, including us.

### Delete anything

Delete a report, a conversation, a person or everything. What a conversation taught Ayumitra goes with it.

### No ads, trackers or account

The app has no advertising, analytics or crash reporting. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers.

## What Ayumitra isn’t

- **A doctor.** It explains your records and helps you prepare for visits. It doesn’t diagnose or treat, and it isn’t a medical device.

- **A source of medicines or doses.** It never suggests a medicine or gives a dose, for anyone, and never tells you to start or stop one. [How the app holds to this](https://ayumitra.app/safety.md#medicines)

- **An emergency service.** If someone is seriously unwell, call your local emergency number.
