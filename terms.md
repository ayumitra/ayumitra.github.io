### Fair use

Pro has a monthly allowance of **2,000 requests** to the AI model, shared by everyone signed in to the account. A question usually takes about three; reading a report or a photo, or Think deeper, can take more. Very long requests, such as Think deeper on long records, use it up faster, so heavy use can reach the allowance before 2,000 requests; on the yearly plan, what very long requests may use builds up month by month, and what a month leaves carries over to the next. Everything Pro does for you draws on it: answers, reading reports and photos, health-memory updates and check-ins. Web searches don’t count. The app shows how much is used. When it is used up, the app answers with the model on your device or your own key until the allowance starts fresh the next month. During the free trial the allowance is **500 requests**; the full allowance starts with the paid subscription.

Pro is made for the health questions of the people in your profiles, so:

- an account can make up to 120 requests an hour and 600 a day, and 50 web searches a day and 150 a month (fewer during the free trial);

- it answers health topics only;

- automated use and reselling are not allowed.

We may slow down or stop an account that abuses the service.
