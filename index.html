Watch it work

Four things Ayumitra does every day, shown with sample records.

Readings come in by themselves

Import once from Health Connect or Apple Health, and new readings from the phone, the watch and the meters you already use keep coming into the right person’s record: blood pressure, blood sugar, oxygen, weight, temperature, heart rate, steps and sleep.

It keeps checking

While Ayumitra is open, it looks for new readings by itself. On Android it can also check about once an hour while it’s closed, if you allow it in Health Connect.

Named, and never twice

Each reading says which app recorded it, and one app’s same reading is kept once. A night two apps both recorded shows under each, and counts once.

Into the right person

Readings go into the profile you choose and are read on the phone only. Nothing is written back. Picked the wrong person? Undo takes the import back.

It says what it understood

The first step says what it took your question to mean. If it can’t tell, it asks you one short question instead of guessing.

A source for every answer

You see each step as it works, and each answer points to the report, reading or conversation it came from. Tap it to open the report at that line.

Warning signs come first

If a message describes warning signs, the app’s own check shows what to do and the number to call, before the answer. That check is built into the app, not left to the AI. It can miss things: if you’re worried, call for help.

Wherever you are

Each person has their own suitcase: while they travel, the emergency numbers shown for them are the country’s they’re in, with WHO’s and CDC’s health advice for it.

Your records are yours to control

There’s no account and no Ayumitra server holding your health records. In practice:

  • Stored on your phone, encrypted

    Records, photos and conversations are kept in an encrypted vault on the phone.

  • Read on your phone

    On a phone that can run it, the AI that reads reports and answers questions runs on the phone itself, and works without a connection.

  • Health apps, read only

    Health Connect and Apple Health are read on the phone, into the profile you choose. Nothing is written back to them.

  • Cloud only if you choose

    You can use OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or OpenRouter with your own key. It stays off until you agree and turn it on. The names of the people in your profiles, and common other spellings of them, are swapped for made-up ones first. Nicknames and other people’s names aren’t changed, and names printed on a photographed report can’t be.

  • See what was sent

    With a cloud model on, Settings shows the exact text sent this session, with the app’s own check for your family’s names in it.

  • A health library in the app

    General questions draw on MedlinePlus’s health topics in English and Spanish, kept in the app and read offline. Web search stays off until you turn it on, and the app lets only a general question through.

  • Shared only between your phones

    A child’s record shared with the other parent goes straight from phone to phone on your Wi‑Fi, encrypted. Chats are never shared.

  • Backups only you can open

    Backups stay on your device for now, encrypted with six words that only you keep. Without them nobody can open a backup, including us.

  • Delete anything

    Delete a report, a conversation, a person or everything. What a conversation taught Ayumitra goes with it.

  • No ads, trackers or account

    The app has no advertising, analytics or crash reporting. This website sets no cookies and loads nothing from other servers.

Why we made Ayumitra

When our son was born, we needed an AI-powered app for his health: one place for his records that could also help us understand them.

We found none that kept a health record and also kept it private. So we built one ourselves.

Ayumitra is that app, now for your family too.

What Ayumitra isn’t

  • A doctor. It explains your records and helps you prepare for visits. It doesn’t diagnose or treat, and it isn’t a medical device.
  • A source of medicines or doses. It never suggests a medicine or gives a dose, for anyone, and never tells you to start or stop one. For a dose it points to the prescription, the label, or your doctor or pharmacist.
  • An emergency service. If someone is seriously unwell, call your local emergency number.

Free, with every feature

No feature will ever be paywalled. All we’ll ever sell is the managed service below and, later, Sanjeevani, our own AI models trained for Ayumitra. Nothing else.
