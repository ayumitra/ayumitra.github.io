# Questions about your data

Short answers to what people ask most. The [privacy policy](https://ayumitra.app/privacy.md) says it in full.

## Where is my data stored?

On your phone or tablet, in an encrypted vault. Its key is kept in the device’s secure storage: we have no copy of the key and no access to the vault.

Without Ayumitra Pro there is no Ayumitra account at all.

## Do you keep my data on a server?

No health records are kept on any Ayumitra server.

With Ayumitra Pro, what you send passes through our server to the AI that answers, and isn’t kept once the answer is back. The Pro account holds only your email address, your plan and this month’s counts of requests and searches.

## What leaves my phone when I ask a question?

It depends on who answers, and you choose:

- **The AI on your phone (Gemma):** your question and your records are read on the phone, and nothing about them is sent. The only exception is what you turn on yourself, such as web search, which sends only general search words.
- **Ayumitra Pro:** your question, and the parts of your records needed to answer it, go through our server to AI models that keep nothing after the answer (zero data retention). The names of the people in your profiles are swapped for made-up ones first.
- **A cloud AI with your own key:** it goes from your phone straight to the provider you chose, under your own account with them. Their terms decide what they keep and how they use it, so check them.

When a cloud model or Pro answers, a small decision model is also asked whether a message may be an emergency and what it needs, and whether the answer says something is yours, so the app can check it against your records before you see it. It gets the message, a little context and the answer’s sentences that name a condition, medicine, allergy or result, through companies that keep nothing after the answer.

## Do you use my data to train AI?

No. We don’t train AI on what you send. Ayumitra Pro uses only AI companies that don’t train on it and keep nothing after the answer. With your own key, your provider’s terms decide.

## Do you sell my data?

No. We don’t sell or share your health records. The app has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking code from other companies.

## What does web search send?

Web search is off until you turn it on. Only general search words are sent: the app checks each search first, and one with a name from your profiles, a date, an age, a reading, an email address, a phone number or a link isn’t sent.

With Pro, searches go through our server to Brave Search, which keeps a record of the search words for up to 90 days and says it can’t tell who made a search. We have asked Brave for zero data retention; until we have it, that record applies. Built-in search asks MedlinePlus and Wikipedia, and a key of your own uses Brave Search or Tavily.

## Can anyone at Ayumitra see my records?

No. Your records are in the encrypted vault on your device, and we have no copy of its key. Backups are encrypted with a six-word passphrase that only you have.

## How do I delete my data?

- **Everything on the device:** Settings › Your data › Delete everything on this device. You can also delete a single report, conversation or profile. Uninstalling the app removes its data too.
- **Backups** you made stay where you saved them until you delete them.
- **A Pro account:** Settings › Ayumitra Pro › Delete Pro account, or the [delete page](https://ayumitra.app/delete-account.md). This doesn’t cancel a running subscription: cancel that in Google Play or the App Store.

## What happens with Health Connect or Apple Health?

Only when you tap Import and allow it, the app reads your readings into the profile you choose. It’s read only: nothing is written back. What it reads stays in the encrypted vault, and goes to a cloud model only if you have turned cloud AI on.

## Is Ayumitra a medical device?

No. It explains your records and helps you prepare for visits. It doesn’t diagnose or treat, and it never suggests a medicine or a dose. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

See [how Ayumitra checks every answer](https://ayumitra.app/safety.md).
